Details

  • Organizer: Andrew Cho.
  • Who can apply: high school students in grades 10, 11 and 12 who do not have a computer of their own and could not otherwise afford one. Teachers, counselors and mentors may nominate a student. One application per student. If the student is under 18, please let a parent or guardian know about the application.
  • Prize: one pre-owned 2015 27" 5K Apple iMac (specs above), donated by the organizer. No cash alternative.
  • Deadline: applications must be submitted by November 30, 2026.
  • How the student is chosen: the organizer will read the applications and choose one student, based on need and on how the student would use the iMac. The decision is final.
  • Notification: the selected student (or their nominator) will be contacted by email on or about December 15, 2026. If there is no response in a reasonable time, another student may be chosen.
  • Handing over the iMac: this will be arranged with the student's parent or guardian, or through the student's school, and not directly with the student alone.
  • Your information: the details you submit are sent to the organizer by email through an online form service. They are used only to review applications and contact the selected student. They will not be sold or shared, and will be deleted after the iMac has been given away.

Applications close November 30, 2026. One student will be selected on December 15, 2026 and notified by email.
